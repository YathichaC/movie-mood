Overview

Stationed aboard the solitary research array Solaris-IV at the edge of a dormant wormhole, astrophysicist Dr. Caleb Vance investigates a series of temporal distortions that begin echoing his lost memories. As cosmic hallucinations threaten crew sanity, the team must decipher whether the surrounding sentient vacuum is unraveling their subjective reality or providing humanity's singular bridge to transcend the spacetime continuum. A cerebral, visually arresting meditation on love, grief, and the infinite abyss.
