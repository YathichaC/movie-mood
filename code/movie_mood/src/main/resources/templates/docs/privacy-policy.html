01

Introduction

Movie Mood is a web application for discovering, searching, and receiving movie recommendations, as well as managing playlists and watch history. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the service.

02

Information We Collect

When you register, Movie Mood collects account information such as your username and email address, along with authentication information. Passwords are stored as hashes rather than plain text.

03

Movie Preferences and Activity

The application may store playlists, saved movies, watch history, and genres you mark as disliked. This information supports personalized features and recommendations.

04

How We Use Information

We use information to create and manage accounts, authenticate users, provide movie search and recommendations, manage playlists and watch history, support password changes and resets, and help maintain application security.

05

Third-Party Services

Movie Mood obtains movie information from The Movie Database (TMDB). The project also integrates with Supabase Storage for file storage and an email service for account-related messages. These third parties may have their own privacy policies.

06

Data Security

The project uses BCrypt password hashing, JWT-based authentication, and time-limited password-reset tokens. These measures help protect information, but no online service can guarantee complete security.

07

Retention and Deletion

Account and feature-related information is retained as needed to operate the service, subject to the actual deployment configuration and applicable requirements. Specific retention periods and backup practices must be confirmed by the operator before publication. Users may manage or delete information using available application features.

08

Your Choices and Rights

Depending on the features available, you can edit profile information, change your password, manage playlists and watch history, and delete your account. Additional privacy rights may apply under relevant law. Contact the service operator to make an applicable request.

09

Younger Users

Younger users should follow appropriate parental guidance and applicable laws. Movie ratings and content classifications may vary according to the information provided by external sources.

10

Changes to This Policy

We may revise this Privacy Policy when application features or relevant requirements change. The current version and its effective date will be displayed on this page.

11

Contact
